2 Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

While using Our Service, We may ask You to provide Us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify You. Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:

  • Email address (when contacting support or using billing services)
  • Usage Data

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service.

Usage Data may include information such as Your Device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address), browser type, browser version, the pages of our Service that You visit, the time and date of Your visit, the time spent on those pages, unique device identifiers and other diagnostic data.

When You access the Service by or through a mobile device, We may collect certain information automatically, including, but not limited to, the type of mobile device You use, Your mobile device unique ID, the IP address of Your mobile device, Your mobile operating system, the type of mobile Internet browser You use, unique device identifiers and other diagnostic data.

Audio and Voice Information

The Application requires access to Your device's microphone to provide its core functionality (voice-to-text reminders). Audio is processed in real-time. Reminder transcripts may be sent through Our backend to an AI processing service for high-accuracy parsing. We do not store raw audio files. We may store reminder transcripts and their structured AI responses for up to 90 days to improve response speed and reliability.

Information Stored Locally

The Application stores Your reminders, tasks, categories, and settings locally in the Application's private storage using a Room database. Device-level encryption and access controls are provided by Android when enabled on Your device. We do not operate a reminder-storage server. Your device OS may include this data in cloud backup or device transfer according to Your Android backup settings.

Use of Your Personal Data

The Company may use Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our Service, including to monitor the usage of our Service.
  • To manage Your requests: To attend and manage Your requests to Us.
  • For the performance of a contract: the development, compliance and undertaking of the purchase contract for the products, items or services You have purchased.
  • To provide You with news, special offers and general information about other goods, services and events which we offer.

3 Permissions

To function correctly, the App requests the following permissions:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used to capture voice input for reminders.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to send you alerts for your scheduled reminders.
  • Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to ensure reminders trigger precisely at the requested time.

4 Third-Party Services and Data Transfer

The Application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. We encourage you to read their privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect data such as advertising IDs (GAID) and device information for personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • Google Play Billing: Used for in-app purchases (Pro Upgrade). Payment processing and purchase status are handled by Google Play. Our backend does not receive billing data, and We do not store Your credit card information.
  • AI processing service: Used to convert natural language into structured reminders. Our backend checks a private, per-installation response cache first and sends the reminder text to the AI service only when a reusable response is unavailable.

5 Retention and Security of Your Data

The Company will retain Your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. Since reminder data is stored locally, it is retained until You delete the reminder or uninstall the Application.

The security of Your Data is important to Us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While We strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect Your Personal Data, We cannot guarantee its absolute security.
