1 Privacy Summary

  • No Application account is required: the Application assigns a random installation identifier for backend AI requests; it is not your advertising ID, email address, or Android account.
  • Reminders are stored locally: saved tasks, dates, times, categories, locations, recurrence details, completion state, and settings are kept in the Application's private storage.
  • Raw audio is not stored by us: voice input is converted to text by the speech-recognition service available on your device. We receive reminder text only when AI parsing is used.
  • AI requests may be cached: prompts and structured responses may be retained temporarily, while reusable templates that exclude the original prompt may be retained longer.
  • The Application displays ads: Google Mobile Ads may process advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, ad interactions, and diagnostics, subject to your consent choices and settings.
  • Analytics: the Application currently disables Firebase Analytics collection. Operational AI request metrics are still recorded as described below.

2 Information We Handle

Information stored on your device

The Application stores reminders and related information locally in a private Room database. This may include task text, date and time, optional location text, category, urgency, recurrence, completion status, alarm state, and preferences such as your selected alarm tone and onboarding state.

Android may include the reminder database and selected Application settings in cloud backup or device-to-device transfer when backup is enabled for your device. Those backups are controlled by your Android and Google account settings.

Voice input

With your permission, the Application uses the microphone for speech-to-text capture. Audio is processed by the speech-recognition service configured on your device, which may process audio on-device or send it to that service's servers. The Application does not create, upload to our backend, or retain raw audio recordings. The resulting text is placed into the reminder input field and is handled like typed text.

Information sent for AI parsing

When AI parsing is available, the Application sends the following to our backend over HTTPS:

  • Reminder input: the text you spoke or typed.
  • Scheduling context: your local date and time, time-zone identifier, and the locally parsed reminder fields needed to interpret your request.
  • Random installation identifier: a generated UUID used for per-installation caching, operational records, reliability, and deletion requests. It is not used to create a public profile.
  • Operational metadata: input length, success or error status, response time, cache result, AI-call status, token counts, and estimated processing cost or savings.
  • Network information: our hosting provider and security systems may process your IP address and request headers for delivery, abuse prevention, rate limiting, and server logs.

Your saved reminder database is not synchronized to our backend. Contact information is collected only if you choose to send it to us for support.

How we use information

  • Provide voice capture, reminder parsing, scheduling, alarms, notifications, snooze, recurrence, and home-screen widget features.
  • Reuse validated responses and generic templates to reduce AI calls, response time, and processing cost.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, measure, and improve the AI service.
  • Display and measure ads according to your consent and privacy choices.
  • Process optional purchases through Google Play and respond to support requests.

3 AI Processing and Response Reuse

Our backend checks stored results before contacting an external AI processing provider. Depending on the request, it may use an exact per-installation match or a generic reusable template. If no sufficiently relevant and valid result exists, the reminder text and scheduling context are sent to the AI provider to produce structured reminder fields.

  • Exact response cache: an eligible prompt, its scheduling context, and structured response are associated with your random installation identifier and are eligible for exact reuse for 30 days.
  • Prompt/response history: cache records and prompts whose meaning changes with time are targeted for deletion after 90 days. Non-cacheable prompts are not reused as exact cached results. Residual copies may remain longer in protected backups or logs until they rotate.
  • Reusable knowledge: generic intent patterns, semantic terms, parameter requirements, and placeholder-based response templates may be retained until they are deactivated or no longer needed. These records are designed to exclude the original prompt, account information, and personal reminder values.
  • Validation: cached or reusable results are checked for required fields, schedule validity, and similarity. Uncertain matches fall back to AI processing.

Please avoid entering secrets or highly sensitive personal information in reminder text. AI output can be inaccurate, so review extracted reminder details before saving.

4 Device Permissions

The Application requests only permissions needed for its current features. You can manage most permissions in Android Settings, but disabling them may prevent related features from working.

  • Microphone: captures speech while you actively use voice input.
  • Internet: connects to our AI backend, loads ads and consent choices, verifies purchases, and checks for Application updates.
  • Notifications: displays scheduled reminder and alarm notifications.
  • Exact alarms: triggers time-sensitive reminders as close as Android permits to the selected time.
  • Full-screen alerts: shows the alarm screen over the lock screen where Android allows it.
  • Foreground media playback: keeps the selected alarm tone playing until you dismiss, complete, or snooze the reminder.
  • Vibration: vibrates the device for reminder alarms when supported.
  • Start after reboot: restores scheduled reminders after restart, Application update, time change, or time-zone change.
  • Google Play Billing: enables optional purchases. Google processes payment details; we do not receive or store your full payment-card information.

5 Ads and Third-Party Services

Third-party providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Information may be processed in countries other than your own.

  • Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform: serve banner and interstitial ads and collect applicable consent choices. Depending on your region, consent, and device settings, Google may process advertising or application identifiers, IP address, device and network information, ad interactions, and diagnostics to deliver, personalize, limit, secure, and measure ads. See how Google uses information from partner apps and Google's advertising information.
  • Google Play: provides billing, purchase status, Application distribution, updates, and Android backup/device transfer. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Device speech-recognition provider: converts microphone input into text according to the recognition service selected on your device and that provider's policy.
  • AI processing provider: receives reminder text and scheduling context only after our backend cannot safely reuse an existing result. We do not send the advertising ID with AI requests.
  • Hosting and database providers: host our backend, database, and security logs and process data only as needed to operate the service.

We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising-related processing may be considered "sharing," "targeted advertising," or a "sale" under some privacy laws. Where required, the Application provides a consent message and a Privacy choices option in Settings. You can also reset or delete your Android advertising ID under your device's privacy settings.

6 Retention and Security

  • Local reminders: remain until you delete them, clear Application data, or uninstall the Application. Android backups may remain according to Google's backup retention rules.
  • Exact AI cache: is eligible for reuse for 30 days.
  • Prompt/response records: are targeted for deletion after 90 days; residual copies may remain longer in protected backups or logs until they rotate.
  • Reusable AI knowledge: may be retained until deactivated or no longer needed because it is designed to contain generic templates rather than original prompt text or personal reminder values.
  • Installation and operational records: may be retained while needed for security, abuse prevention, service measurement, troubleshooting, legal obligations, and deletion auditing, then deleted or aggregated.
  • Support communications: are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain appropriate business records.

We use HTTPS in production, access controls, request-size limits, validation, rate limiting, and restricted database access. No transmission or storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7 Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection, and to appeal or withdraw consent. We will verify and respond to requests as required by applicable law.

  • Review and edit AI-extracted details before saving a reminder.
  • Delete individual or multiple reminders in the Application, or clear all local Application data through Android Settings.
  • Manage microphone, notification, alarm, and full-screen permissions through Android Settings.
  • Open Settings > Privacy choices in the Application to review available advertising consent options.
  • Contact us to request access to or deletion of backend information associated with your installation. We may need information from your device to identify the applicable installation record.

8 Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Application, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate. Parents and guardians should supervise reminder text because it may be processed by advertising, speech-recognition, and AI service providers.
