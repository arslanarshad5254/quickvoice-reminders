## 📖 Long Description (Max 4000 characters)

**Transform your voice into action with QuickVoice Reminders—the fastest way to stay organized on Android.**

Tired of typing out every single task? QuickVoice uses the power of **Gemini AI** to understand your natural speech and schedule reminders in under 3 seconds. Simply speak or type what you need to do, and our smart engine handles the rest.

### 🎙️ VOICE-FIRST PRODUCTIVITY
Whether you say *"Remind me to call the doctor tomorrow at 10:30 AM"* or *"Buy milk after 10 minutes"*, QuickVoice extracts the task, date, and time accurately. No more fiddling with manual date pickers.

### 🤖 HYBRID AI & OFFLINE PARSING
* **Gemini AI Powered:** For complex requests, our AI engine identifies locations, categories, and urgency.
* **Resilient Offline NLP:** No internet? No problem. Our high-speed local parser ensures your reminders are saved instantly even in "Airplane Mode".

### ⚡ NEVER FORGET WITH NAG MODE
For your most critical tasks, enable **Nag Mode**. The app will persistently alert you every 5 minutes until you mark the task as complete. Perfect for high-priority deadlines and urgent errands.

### 🛠️ POWERFUL FEATURES
* **Smart Dashboard:** View your tasks with metric badges for Pending, Today, Urgent, and Done.
* **Home Screen Widget:** Access your reminders at a glance and trigger voice recording with a single tap using Android Glance.
* **Snooze & Sync:** Snooze reminders by 10 minutes directly from the notification—snoozing automatically updates the task time in your dashboard.
* **Custom Notification Tones:** Personalize your experience by choosing your favorite alert sounds.
* **Undo Delete:** Accidentally swiped a task? Restore it instantly with one tap.
* **Material 3 Design:** A sleek, modern dark-themed interface built for the latest Android standards.
